# Vantorflow Acceptable Use Policy (draft)

This policy applies to all Vantorflow accounts and features. Questions or reports: support@vantorflow.com.ng.

Users must use Vantorflow lawfully and responsibly. You may not:
- access, probe, scrape, disrupt or attempt to bypass another account, system or security control;
- evade quotas, rate limits, account restrictions or abuse detection, or automate excessive requests;
- upload malware, exploit code or content intended to compromise a person or system;
- use the service for fraud, deception, impersonation, unlawful discrimination, harassment, or other illegal conduct;
- submit content you do not have permission to use, or expose confidential/personal data without authority;
- rely on generated content without human review for accuracy, compliance, pricing, deadlines or bid submission;
- reverse engineer or interfere with the service except where applicable law does not permit restriction.

We may apply reasonable rate limits, investigate reports, create security flags, request verification, restrict usage, suspend or terminate accounts, and preserve or disclose information as legally required. Enforcement should be proportionate and reviewable. Automated signals can be mistaken; users may contact support@vantorflow.com.ng to request review. Do not publish final thresholds or detection details that would facilitate evasion.

This is a draft. Align it with the Terms, privacy notice, actual product controls and applicable law before publication.
