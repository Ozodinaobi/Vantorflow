# Vantorflow Privacy Policy (draft)

**Effective date:** to be set before publication. **Controller/service provider:** legal entity and address to be completed. Privacy and support contact: support@vantorflow.com.ng.

This draft describes the intended handling of information through Vantorflow. Confirm the actual production systems, processors, retention and legal basis before publishing. It is not legal advice.

## Information we handle
Depending on use, Vantorflow may process account information such as name, email, authentication and verification status; workspace records such as RFQs, opportunities, requirements and generated drafts; support messages; usage, security and audit events; and technical data such as device/browser details, IP-derived security signals and logs. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless necessary and authorized.

## Purposes and legal basis
We use information to create and secure accounts, provide the platform, store and retrieve work, operate AI-assisted features, prevent misuse, respond to support requests, maintain service reliability, comply with legal obligations and improve the product. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, identify and document the applicable basis for each purpose before launch, including contract, legitimate interests, consent or legal obligation as appropriate.

## AI features and service providers
Submitted content may be sent to infrastructure and AI providers to perform requested features. Identify each provider, its role, processing location, retention and training settings in a published subprocessor list before launch. Do not claim providers never retain or train on data until contracts and configuration have been verified. We do not sell personal information under this draft.

## Sharing and international transfers
Information may be shared with hosting, database, email, AI, security and support providers under appropriate safeguards, or disclosed to authorities where legally required. Services may process data outside your country. Complete provider names, locations and transfer mechanisms, and any required notices or assessments, before publication.

## Retention and security
Keep data only as long as needed for the stated purposes, legal requirements, dispute resolution and backup cycles. Publish actual retention periods for account data, content, usage/security logs, support records and backups. We use access controls and security measures appropriate to the service, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Define an incident response and notification process.

## Your choices and rights
Depending on location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a regulator. Contact support@vantorflow.com.ng to submit a request. We may verify identity and apply lawful exceptions, including records needed for security, legal obligations or disputes. Document a response timeline and identity-verification process before launch.

## Cookies and similar technology
Strictly necessary session/security cookies support sign-in and platform operation. Optional analytics or advertising cookies must not run before valid consent where required. Publish the actual cookie inventory, purposes, providers, expiry and withdrawal controls. The planned cookie banner is not yet a substitute for a tested consent-management system.

## Children
The service is intended for business users and is not designed for children. Add the applicable minimum age and handling process after legal review.

## Changes and contact
We may update this notice and will communicate material changes where required. Questions, rights requests and privacy concerns: support@vantorflow.com.ng. Add the legal entity name, postal address, applicable regulator/complaint route and any Data Protection Officer details if required.
